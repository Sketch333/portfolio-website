## Overview

This analytics project covers a multivariate sensor time-series workflow spanning cleaning, denoising, device profiling, K-means clustering, rolling features, anomaly detection, health scoring, forecasting, and cross-device similarity.

## Evidence

The [project README](https://github.com/Sketch333/Industrial_IoT_Sensor_Analytics/blob/main/README.md) documents the anomaly methods shown in the walkthrough. It also lists device profiling, K-means clustering, rolling features, cross-device similarity, and forecasting approaches. The repository includes a notebook and output artifacts; this page does not claim measured performance or a live sensor feed.
